Brand Guidelines

The brand

Full Desk Leads has no symbol. The name is the mark. Three words closed into one, capitals raised so the eye still reads each of them, and a motto ruled in beneath like a line on an engraved instrument.

Positioning

The premier lead source to scale your recruiting business. Built for firms that win on the volume of qualified hiring-manager conversations.

Personality

Premium. Prestigious. Simple. Modern. Confident without swagger — the tone of a firm that already has the accounts.

The motto

Recruit & Conquer. Always an ampersand. Always uppercase at 0.23em tracking. Never re-typed by hand — use the files.

How the wordmark is built

Three decisions define it, and all three are load-bearing.

Closed, not spaced

The words run together. Spaced, it reads as a description of a product. Closed, it reads as a name — and it matches the domain exactly, so anyone who sees it already knows where to go.

Capitals at 132%

F, D and L are set a third larger than the lowercase. They replace the spaces as word separators and give the mark its rhythm.

Weight compensated

Enlarging a capital in a high-contrast serif thickens its stems. The capitals are therefore drawn at a lighter weight (450) than the lowercase (600) so the strokes optically match.

That last point is why the wordmark cannot be reproduced by typing the name and scaling the capitals. It is a drawn artefact. Always place the supplied file.

Primary lockup

The wordmark with the motto ruled beneath it. This is the default signature and should be the first choice anywhere there is room for it.

PrimaryHomepage, marketing, decks, letterhead, formal documents.

Wordmark

The motto removed. Use when the mark appears more than once on a surface, when the motto would fall below legible size, or in product interfaces where a motto reads as marketing intruding on a tool.

WordmarkNav bars, app headers, repeat placements.
Wordmark, reversedDark navigation, footers, merchandise.

Stacked lockup

For narrow and square placements where the horizontal lockup would have to shrink below its minimum — sidebars, tall banners, square advertising, apparel backs, signage.

StackedNarrow columns, square formats, print.
Stacked, reversedSquare social posts, apparel, signage.

Small-space marks

The wordmark cannot survive a 16-pixel square, so three reduced marks exist. Use the largest one that fits, and never invent a fourth.

Stacked tileProfile pictures, 400 px and above.
FDL monogramApp icons and avatars, 48–400 px.
Letter markFavicon and browser tab, 16–48 px.
Letter, on lightWatermarks, stationery detail.

The favicon letter is drawn heavier than the wordmark's capitals. A high-contrast serif loses its thin strokes below roughly 24 pixels, so the small cut is thickened to survive. This is deliberate, and the two files are not interchangeable.

Clear space and minimum sizes

Maintain clear space of 1x on all four sides, where x is the cap height of the F. Nothing enters that zone — no type, no rules, no photographic edges, no other logos.

Below 200 px wide, drop the motto and use the wordmark. Below 130 px, use the stacked lockup or a small-space mark.

Typography

Display — Playfair Display

Forty conversations a week

Recruit & Conquer

Weights 450, 500 and 600. Headlines, the wordmark, the motto, pull quotes. Never body copy.

Text — Inter

Interface and body

Inter carries everything functional: product UI, tables, buttons, forms, and long-form copy. Set body at 400 and interface labels at 500.

Eyebrow / label style

Uppercase Inter 500 at 0.14em tracking, for section labels and table headers.

Usage

Do

Place the supplied files exactly as provided.

Use the green mark on ivory, white, or very light neutrals.

Use the white mark on green, charcoal, or a dark photographic area.

Scale proportionally, always from a corner.

Use the charcoal cut for one-colour print, engraving and fax.

Keep the full clear space, even in cramped layouts.

Don't

Re-type the name and scale the capitals by hand.

Re-space the words or insert gaps between them.

Add gradients, bevels, glows, outlines or drop shadows.

Recolour outside the five approved colourways.

Stretch, condense, rotate or skew the mark.

Add .com — the closed wordmark already is the domain.

Add a symbol, icon or emblem alongside it.

For the build team

Import the tokens once and reference the variables rather than pasting hex values. Inline the SVG where it needs to inherit colour or animate; otherwise reference the file.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/brand/developer/tokens.css">

.site-header  { background: var(--fdl-ivory); }
.site-header h1 { font-family: var(--fdl-font-display); color: var(--fdl-green); }
.btn-primary  { background: var(--fdl-green); color: var(--fdl-white);
                border-radius: var(--fdl-radius); font-family: var(--fdl-font-text); }
.divider      { border-top: 1px solid var(--fdl-hairline); }

Every wordmark in this kit is converted to vector outlines. Nothing depends on a font being installed on the viewer's machine, and no text inside a logo file can be reflowed or substituted by a browser.
